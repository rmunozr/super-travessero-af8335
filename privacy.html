Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 5, 2026

Deep Finance Lab uses the information submitted through its website assistant only to answer enquiries and manage appointments.

Information we process

We may process your name, email address, telephone number, selected service, professional, appointment time, messages and booking reference.

How we use information

We use this information to identify your appointment records, check availability, create or cancel appointments, send confirmations and requested appointment reminders, respond to enquiries, protect the service and diagnose operational errors.

Service providers

The assistant may use Netlify for hosting and scheduled processing, Google Sheets for client and appointment records, Google Calendar and Gmail for scheduling and email, xAI for conversational responses, and Meta WhatsApp Business Platform for appointment messages. Each provider processes data under its own terms and privacy commitments.

Google user data

Access to Google user data is limited to client and appointment records in a designated spreadsheet, calendar availability and event management for designated Deep Finance Lab calendars, and sending appointment-related email. Google user data is not sold, used for advertising or used to train generalized AI models.

Retention and security

Appointment information, reminder delivery status and a limited operational audit trail are retained only as reasonably necessary to operate the service, meet legal obligations and resolve disputes. Contact identifiers used internally are pseudonymized where practical. Access credentials are stored as encrypted hosting secrets and are not exposed to website visitors.

Appointment communications

When you provide an email address or WhatsApp number for a booking, Deep Finance Lab may use that channel for transactional confirmations and reminders about that appointment. You may request that future reminders be stopped by contacting us.

Your choices

You may request access, correction or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable legal requirements, by contacting dfl@deepfinancelab.com.

Changes

We may update this policy as the service evolves. The effective date above identifies the current version.
